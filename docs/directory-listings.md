# Directory listings: KeepYourStay

Ready-to-paste text for business directories. Each listing gives you a link back to your site and keeps your name, phone and website consistent across the web. That consistency also helps your Google Business Profile.

**Time needed:** about 20–30 minutes per directory. Do the four "Start here" listings this week.

---

## Rules (to stay honest and avoid removal)

- **Use the exact same details everywhere.** Copy them from the "Core details" table below, character for character.
- **Never** post fake reviews, buy reviews or ask friends to review you. Clutch and GoodFirms verify reviews by phone or LinkedIn and remove fake ones.
- **Portfolio:** label the Juniper & Tide demo and the other concepts as **"Concept project / illustrative design"** in the title or description. Never present them as client work.
- **Pricing fields:** some directories require an hourly rate or a minimum project size. Enter the real figures you would quote. Directories show these as ranges, not fixed prices, which matches your site. If a field is optional, leave it blank.
- **Team size / founded:** be truthful. "1–9 employees" and the real year are fine. Small and honest beats big and fake.

## Where to list

### Start here (do these four first)

| # | Directory | Why it matters | How to sign up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bing Places for Business** | Bing, ChatGPT search and Copilot use Bing's business data | Go to bingplaces.com → sign in → **Import from Google Business Profile**. It copies everything in a few clicks. |
| 2 | **Apple Business Connect** | Puts you on Apple Maps and Siri | Go to businessconnect.apple.com → sign in with an Apple ID → add the business. Use the same service-area and hidden-address settings as Google. |
| 3 | **LinkedIn company page** | Strong profile link, and needed for Clutch review verification | LinkedIn → **For Business** → **Create a Company Page**. Use the long description, logo and cover. |
| 4 | **Clutch** | The biggest agency directory; buyers search it | Go to clutch.co → **Get Listed** (free). The profile goes live after review. Ask real clients for verified reviews later. |

## Watch out for

- **"Featured" or "top agency" upsells.** Directories will email you offering paid placement. You don't need it to get started; free profiles still give the link and consistency.
- **Award badges you have to pay for.** Skip them. They don't help with Google, and paid "awards" can look misleading to clients.
- **Duplicate listings.** If a directory has already auto-created a KeepYourStay profile, claim it instead of creating a second one.
