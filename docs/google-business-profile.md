## 1. Stay eligible (do this first)

Google only allows profiles for businesses that meet customers in person. Online-only businesses can be suspended.

- Set the profile up as a **service-area business**: **Business information → Location → hide address**, or leave it blank.
- Under **Service areas**, list only the city or county around where you're based, where you would genuinely meet a client. Do **not** add "United States", "Worldwide" or far-away cities.
- Only show **hours** when someone really answers. Example: Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00. Leave weekends closed unless you work them.

## 7. Reviews

Reviews are the biggest local ranking factor you control. Google's rules are strict:

- Ask **only real clients**, after the work is done. Use the profile's **Ask for reviews** link.
- **Never** offer discounts or gifts for reviews, write them yourself, or post them for others. Google removes these and can suspend the profile. The FTC can also fine businesses for fake reviews.
- Reply to **every** review within a few days, good or bad, briefly and personally.
- The site's private `/feedback` page is a separate thing, for on-site testimonials. You can send clients both links.

## 8. Q&A, messages and upkeep
